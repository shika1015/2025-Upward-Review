Strengths & Concerns — Key Themes from Open-Ended Responses

Nicolas / Joshua / Mahadi / Andrea — Strengths to continue

Calmness, patience, fairness, and approachability consistently praised. Nicolas in particular received multiple comments highlighting that he "never gets upset no matter the situation" and "engages with everyone with dignity regardless of their position." Mahadi achieved perfect 5.0 scores on Q7 and Q8. Andrea is recognized for assigning work that plays to each person's individual strengths and weaknesses.

Cora — Serious concerns (score 15.75 / 45 · 35% achievement)

Multiple independent respondents reported power harassment, micromanagement, favoritism, and racial bias. A lack of psychological safety — "difficult to speak up because of her close relationship with someone in power" — was a recurring theme. Q5 (fairness) averaged 1.25 and Q7 (team environment) averaged 1.25, both the lowest scores across all managers. Requires urgent attention.

Chiqui — Needs improvement (score 28.38 · 63% achievement)

Q5 (fairness) averaged 2.77. Multiple respondents cited spending work time on personal matters, a reluctance to give direct feedback, and a tendency to insist on her own view even when challenged. Notably, she also received the most responses of any manager (13), adding weight to the pattern. Some positive comments about her reliability and kindness suggest leadership skill development could be effective.

Lans — Below average (score 26.67 · 59% achievement)

Q3 (communication) scored 1.67 — notably low. Respondents noted a tendency to take over tasks rather than coach, limiting opportunities for team members to develop autonomy and problem-solving skills.

Nicolas — Constructive feedback (high scores, but specific areas raised)

Some respondents noted a tendency to over-index on past mistakes during evaluations rather than reflecting recent growth. There were also calls for more consistent rule enforcement regardless of personal relationships. Separately, feedback on Joshua flagged over-reliance on Nicolas for decisions, with encouragement to build independent judgment.
